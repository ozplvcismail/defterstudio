This text describes how the Garanti Defteri app (iOS and Android) handles data. It is based on the actual behaviour of the app code.

1. In short

  • The app does not ask for an account and does not sign you in.
  • Your data is stored on your device. The app has no server of its own.
  • The app does not collect your personal data and does not send it to the developer or to any third party.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking tools are used.
  • The app does not make network requests on its own. External links open only when you tap them, in the system browser.

2. Data processed and where it is kept

The information you enter (product name, brand, model, serial number, purchase and delivery dates, seller, service phone number, warranty and service notes) and the photos and PDF files you add are kept only in the app's private folder on your device. This data is not sent to the developer.

3. Permissions and what they are used for

  • Camera: used only to take a photo of a document. On iOS it is requested with the system permission on first use; on Android the system camera app opens and this app does not request a separate camera permission. You can always add documents from your gallery or with the file picker.
  • Photos (gallery): used only for the photo you select to add as a document.
  • File picker: used only for the PDF document you select.
  • Notifications: used to show local reminders when a warranty is close to ending, and when the maximum repair time of an open service record is close to running out. Notification text includes the product name and may appear on the lock screen. Reminders are shown approximately and may be delayed. If you decline, the app keeps working and reminders are not shown.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone or calendar permissions.

4. Export, import and sharing

  • Export: the "Export" action gathers all your data and documents into one .gdefteri file. This file is not encrypted and may contain personal documents. It goes to the app or location you choose in the share sheet. The app does not send this file itself. You are responsible for where you save it and who you share it with.
  • Import: reads the .gdefteri file you select and replaces the current data with it. Before importing, an automatic backup of your current data is saved in the app's own folder. If you do not share that backup, it stays on your device.
  • Sharing (product PDF summary, documents): the system share sheet opens only when you tap "Share".

5. Backups and device changes

  • The app has no cloud backup or sync of its own.
  • Android: the database and documents are not included in Google cloud backup. Whether device-to-device transfer carries the data depends on the device and the Android version; when changing phones, use the file you saved with ‘Export’.
  • iOS: your device backup (iCloud or computer backup, depending on your settings) may include the app data. These backups are controlled by Apple and your account, not by the developer.

6. Deleting data

  • You can delete products, documents and service records inside the app. Deleted records and files are removed from the device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the app's data from the device. Files you exported and copies you shared are not covered by this; you manage them yourself.
  • Because there is no developer server holding your data, no separate deletion request channel is needed.
  • Device backups made earlier (iCloud or computer) may remain in your own account. Diagnostic data collected by Apple and Google depends on your device settings and is outside this app.

7. Third-party links

Official source links in the app (Law No. 6502, Warranty Certificate Regulation, After-Sales Services Regulation, Ministry of Trade of the Republic of Türkiye) open in the system browser only when you tap them. The privacy policies of those websites are their own responsibility and are not covered by this policy. The "Call" action opens your phone's dialer with the saved service number; you start the call yourself.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children. Its target audience is users aged 18 and over, and the app is designed for that audience. The app collects no personal data, so no data is collected from children.

9. Informational content

Warranty, maximum repair time and holiday calculations in the app are estimates and are for information only. They are not legal advice. For binding rights and outcomes, consult the competent authorities and the legislation.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and the effective date is updated. If the app's data behaviour changes, this policy is updated first.
