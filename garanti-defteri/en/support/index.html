I am not getting notifications

  • In the app, check that Settings > Notifications is switched on. If Settings shows "System notification permission is off", tap "Open system settings" and allow notifications for Garanti Defteri in your phone's settings.
  • Reminders are attempted about 30 and 7 days before a warranty ends, and before the maximum repair time of an open service record runs out, at around 9:00. Timing is approximate; battery saving, Do Not Disturb or operating system restrictions may delay or block a notification.
  • No notification is shown for a product whose end date or reminder day has already passed.
  • Opening the app now and then helps keep scheduled reminders up to date.
  • Notifications are the app's only way of reminding you; also check important dates on the product screen.

I am changing phones

  • The app has no account or cloud sync; your data may not move to the new phone on its own.
  • On the old phone, use Settings > Export to save all your data and documents in one .gdefteri file, and keep the file somewhere you trust. This file is not encrypted and contains personal documents.
  • On the new phone, install the app and pick that file with Settings > Import. Importing replaces the data currently on the new phone; an automatic backup of the current data is saved in the app's folder first.
  • On Android, the data is not included in Google cloud backup. Whether device-to-device transfer carries the data depends on the device and the Android version. On iOS, your device backup (iCloud or computer) may include the app data. In both cases the export file is the most reliable way.

Where is my data stored?

  • The information, photos and PDF documents you add are kept only in the app's folder on your phone. The app has no server; your data is not sent to the developer.
  • The app does not ask for an account and uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • If you uninstall the app, its data is removed from the device as well. Export before uninstalling.
  • For details, see the privacy policy: https://ozplvcismail.github.io/defterstudio/garanti-defteri/en/

What does "Estimated" mean?

  • Results such as the warranty end date, the business days left and the repair days added to the warranty are calculated from the dates you entered and the official holiday list in the app. That is why they carry the "Estimated" label; the actual period may differ.
  • For the maximum repair time counter, Saturdays and half-day holiday eves count as business days; Sundays, public holidays and religious holidays do not.
  • The holiday list covers 2026 and 2027. In years outside that range religious holidays are not taken into account, and the app says so on screen.
  • If dates were entered incorrectly, or the seller or manufacturer gave a different period, the result changes. You can edit the warranty period and the maximum repair time for each product.
